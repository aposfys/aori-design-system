## The five rules that matter most

1. **Corner radius is zero, and there are no shadows.** Depth is a 2px carbon rule, a 1px
   hairline, or a flat pigment field. Never a blur.
2. **Terracotta acts, aegean links, saffron highlights.** Four to six colours per
   composition, laid flat and opaque. Never a gradient.
3. **Motifs are circles, patterns are squares**, and a motif is never redrawn — every one is
   cut out of a finished painting.
4. **A painting opens a page; a photograph shows a piece.** A photograph never opens the
   site and never appears on packaging. Photographs go full bleed with no border; paintings
   keep a 5px unpainted margin on toned stock.
5. **Numbers are load-bearing.** Twelve in a run, four left, silver 925, 24 mm. State them
   plainly. Scarcity is stated, never sold: "All twelve gone", not "Selling fast".

## Before you build

- Read `README.md` in full — the photography question is genuinely unresolved and the
  answer changes what you are allowed to do with product imagery.
- `assets/photography/` holds generated placeholder tiles, not photographs — the
  free-licence stand-ins the guidelines reference are not in this repository. Never present
  one as a product shot; substitute a painting, or say plainly that the image is missing.
- Collections, prices and run counts are invented placeholder data.
- Greek script belongs to the brand book only. Product surfaces are Latin.
- The wordmark in `assets/logos/wordmark.svg` is the only mark. Do not invent one, and do
  not substitute type-set letters for it above 28px.
