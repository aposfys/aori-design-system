AORI is a design system, not a brand: the rules are the system's, and the worked examples speak in the voice of its flagship case — a handmade-jewellery workshop in Heraklion, real on Instagram, its product data invented and labelled so. A harbourside taverna is built from the same tokens to prove the system carries across products.

Everything visual is watercolour and gouache on cotton paper. Corner radius is zero, there are no shadows, and depth is a 2px carbon rule, a 1px hairline, or a flat pigment field — never a blur.

This book is generated from the repository — docs/brandbook/book.html is its source, and the live pages under guidelines/ carry the same rules with working demonstrations. Where the book compresses, the guideline page it names is the authority.

We make twelve. When they are gone we make something else.
AORI — Brand Book02
01 · Philosophy

A workshop, not a brand studio

The system exists to make one thing possible: a shop, a document or a post that looks like it came from a workshop rather than a brand studio. Its centre of gravity is the bench — things are made in small numbers, by hand, and the visual language keeps that honesty: paint instead of photography-gloss, arithmetic instead of superlatives, paper instead of chrome.

Three materials carry everything. The paintings — watercolour and gouache on cotton paper — are the loudest thing in the system and take the romance. The clerical sheet — stone ground, form-green ink, hairlines — takes the evidence: sizes, prices, shipping, specification. The carbon line draws everything functional: rules, icons, and the drawn register.

Every layout is a negotiation between those three, and the negotiation has rules — that is what the rest of this book is.

AORI — Brand Book · Philosophy03
01 · Voice

The voice is a maker talking

  • PersonFirst person plural for the workshop, second person for the reader. Never "AORI is proud to present".
  • CasingSentence case in prose. Uppercase only in the annotation voice — labels, buttons, metadata — where it is always tracked. Display headings are uppercase and tracked; body copy never is.
  • Numbers are load-bearingTwelve in a run. Four left. Silver 925. Two working days. The brand's honesty is arithmetic; numbers are stated plainly and never rounded up for effect.
  • Scarcity is stated, never sold"Four left of twelve", not "Selling fast". A closed run says "All twelve gone" and keeps its page — the piece stays visible; only the price goes.
  • No exclamation marks. No emoji.The one ornamental flourish in the brand is the decorative unicode rule in the Instagram bio, and it belongs to the bio only.
  • Greek is reserved for the brand bookProduct surfaces are Latin only. Place names keep their Latin spelling: Psiloritis, Livyko, Elia, Knosos.
Verbatim, from the system
Made by hand in Heraklion, in runs of twelve, and named after the places they came from.
Silver 925, hammered on the bench and left matte. Named for the beach below the village, where the water goes flat at six in the evening.
We will make more, but not the same.

The register test: if a sentence could open a perfume advert, it fails. If it could be said across the bench, plainly, to one customer, it passes.

02 · Logotype

One mark, painted once

  • The only markThe wordmark is a vector trace of painted lettering and is the only mark. It is never re-set in type, never redrawn, and no second mark may be invented.
  • Emblems sit under itAn emblem may seal a box or close a page, but it never replaces the name.
  • Four colourways, cut plain and with the macronThe choice is decided by the ground, never by taste: ink on paper (the default, everywhere), terracotta on stock (packaging, cards), paper on pigment (hero bands only), paper on carbon (the media kit). The macron cut and the plain cut never appear together on one surface.
  • One per surfaceA page, a post, a box face carries the mark once.

04 · Typography

Setting the page

  • MeasureProse measures 62ch; annotation columns 46ch. A line of body serif wider than that stops being a letter and starts being a wall.
  • Hierarchy is three levelsA display heading, an annotation label, body prose. If a layout needs a fourth level, it is two layouts.
  • Prices are displayA price is set in GFS Didot with the name, never in the annotation voice — the number is part of the product's name, not its metadata.
  • The clerical registerForm labels are italic Source Serif or tracked annotation caps on stone; values are body serif. Nothing in the ledger is bold except an emphasised total.
  • NumeralsTabular figures wherever numbers align — run counts, prices in a table, the line sheet.

05 · Illustration

The painted hand

Saturated fields held inside a drawn contour, the paper reading through the lighter passages, nothing opaque white, nothing greyed to recede. The full plate mounts on toned stock with a 5px unpainted margin, so it never floats — a painting is an object laid on the page, not a background.

  • One handEvery plate is the same hand. Commission new plates in it, or a new product starts from this library — the palette comes from the paintings, not the other way round.
  • A painting opens the siteA photograph never does. A collection tile is a place, so it is a painting; a product tile is a piece, so it is a photograph.

Motifs are circles, patterns are squares

The six lifted details — decoration with provenance
  • Provenance is the ruleEach is cut out of a finished painting and belongs to a place — using one is a claim about which place a page is about.
  • LimitsMinimum 40px (below it the wash muddies — use nothing instead). One motif per page or post. Never redrawn, never a control.
Three patterns, three strengths
Behind long prose the pattern falls to 8% — present as texture, invisible as image, and the text never loses a point of contrast.

05c · Illustration

The drawn register

  • The third job of the one handEight bench drawings on a 96px grid at the icons' 2px carbon rule, one pigment accent each. Not paintings — they claim no provenance from the plates.
  • Service, not romanceEmpty states, care and sizing instructions, transactional email, booking confirmations. A drawing never opens a page and never sits beside a painted plate — the painting wins.
  • Sizes and colour96px standard, 64px minimum, 160px maximum. Never recoloured, never given a background of its own.
  • Words beside itThe annotation line under a drawing says the thing plainly: "All twelve gone." "Keep above the water."
  • The motif law holdsNo drawn octopus, no drawn sun, no drawn spiral. Motifs stay paintings; drawings depict objects from the bench and the shop.

05b · Photography

The photography problem

The paintings are the loudest thing in the system: maximum chroma, hard contour, white paper. Every treatment that makes a photograph quieter moves it further from them. So the question is not "what filter" — it is which of four routes the workshop commits to. The decision is still open, documented in full at guidelines/photo-blending.html.

Route 1
Paintings are the product imagery

Photographs live only on a plain spec panel.

Cost: free
Route 2
Have the pieces painted

Send the artist the actual jewellery.

Cost: artist time
Route 3 · recommended now
Shoot on the painted paper

Lay pieces on a real painted sheet in daylight.

Cost: one afternoon
Route 4
Make the split the identity

Painted world for romance, clerical sheet for evidence.

Cost: free

Recommendation: route 3 now, route 2 over time. Nine positions on how a photograph may sit beside a painting are rendered live at guidelines/illustration-library.html §04.

Untreated regardless of route: sizing and fit shots, the one detail shot showing hallmark and finish, and anything sent to a stockist or a magazine. Photographs go full bleed with no border — a border reads as a frame, and the brand does not frame things. The eight photography paths in this repository hold generated placeholder tiles that say so on their face.

07 · Motion

Restrained to the point of near-absence

Durations

base · fades220ms
slow420ms

Opacity and position only, standard ease. No bounce, no spring, no scale-on-hover, no parallax, no scroll-triggered reveals. Reduced motion collapses every duration to zero.

Interactive states
  • HoverThe underline appears, or opacity lifts .62 → 1. On a pigment field the pigment darkens one step — never lightens.
  • PressDarkens one more step and travels 1px. Nothing shrinks.
  • FocusA 2px deep-water outline, offset 3px so it lands on the paper. Never removed.
  • Disabledtext-faint on paper-300, no pointer — the one job text-faint has.

09 · In use

The workshop, across six channels

Screen, print, social and email, every one composed from the same tokens, registers and rules: paintings for the places, photographs for the pieces, the clerical sheet for the arithmetic, and the drawn register for the service moments. Nothing is styled per-channel; the channel only chooses which registers it needs.

Sources live in cases/ and render from styles.css alone. Every collection, price and run count is invented and labelled placeholder — the honesty rules of the voice apply to the system's own documentation too.

09 · In use

The proof — a second product, nothing reskinned

The taverna is entirely fictional, and that is the point: it was built from the same tokens, registers and rules with nothing reskinned. The palette came from the paintings; the display face carries the menu the way it carries the runs; the ledger register prices the fish the way it prices the silver.

  • What transferred untouchedType, colour, icons, rules, spacing, motion, components — the entire token layer and everything built on it.
  • What each product suppliesIts own paintings (or a commission in the same hand), its own words in the maker's voice, its own arithmetic.
  • The recipeStart from this library, let the palette come from the paintings, keep the registers. If a choice needs a new rule, the rule joins the system — not the product.

10 · Governance

How the system stays honest

Tokens are the source of truth

194 custom properties in tokens/*.css — colour, type, spacing, shape, elevation, motion, patterns, surfaces. tools/build_tokens.py regenerates the machine-readable tokens.json; the CSS is edited, the JSON never is.

$ python3 tools/build_tokens.py
tokens/tokens.json: 194 tokens
$ python3 tools/check_contrast.py
All 18 pairs clear. The gate is open.
The gate

Every colour role is a declared pair with a WCAG bar (page 12). The gate re-measures all of them on every run and fails the build when a token edit puts one under its bar. Add a pair whenever a token takes on a new text job — the list is the accessibility contract.

Agents are first-class

SKILL.md makes the repository a Claude Code skill; every component's .prompt.md is its contract for generated use.

Open decisions
  • Photography route1, 2, 3 or 4 (page 21). Blocks the shop pages from being real.
  • Licensed facesThe three faces are Google-hosted substitutions; licensed files swap in via tokens/fonts.css alone.
  • The wordmark originalThe trace is used flat; the full-size painted artwork is still wanted.
  • Three unpainted motifsA spiral, a hoop and a beaded strand painted as marks would complete the disc set.
