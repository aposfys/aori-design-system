AORI is a design system, not a brand: the rules are the system's, and the worked examples speak in the voice of its flagship case — a handmade-jewellery workshop in Heraklion, real on Instagram, its product data invented and labelled so. A harbourside taverna is built from the same tokens to prove the system carries across products.

Everything visual is watercolour and gouache on cotton paper. Corner radius is zero, there are no shadows, and depth is a 2px carbon rule, a 1px hairline, or a flat pigment field — never a blur.

We make twelve. When they are gone we make something else.

01 · Philosophy

The voice is a maker talking

  • PersonFirst person plural for the workshop, second person for the reader. Never "AORI is proud to present".
  • CasingSentence case in prose. Uppercase only in the annotation voice — labels, buttons, metadata — where it is always tracked. Display headings are uppercase and tracked; body copy never is.
  • Numbers are load-bearingTwelve in a run. Four left. Silver 925. Two working days. The brand's honesty is arithmetic; numbers are stated plainly and never rounded up for effect.
  • Scarcity is stated, never sold"Four left of twelve", not "Selling fast". A closed run says "All twelve gone" and keeps its page.
  • No exclamation marks. No emoji.Greek is reserved for the brand book; product surfaces are Latin only.
Verbatim, from the system
Made by hand in Heraklion, in runs of twelve, and named after the places they came from.

02 · Logotype

One wordmark, and its subordinates

  • The only markThe wordmark — a vector trace of painted lettering supplied by the workshop — is the only logo. No mark was invented for this system.
  • Emblems sit under itThe five painted emblem files contain no readable brand name; they are filed as emblems and always sit subordinate to the wordmark, never in its place.
  • ColourThe wordmark runs in ink, terracotta, aegean, olive or paper — the palette and nothing outside it. Never on a photograph, never over a pattern at full strength.
  • Space and minimumsClear space, minimum sizes, placement and the eight misuses are drawn in full at guidelines/logotype.html.

The scale runs 11 / 13 / 15 / 17 / 20 / 25 / 32 / 42 / 58 / 80 / 112px. Wide tracking on display type is the loudest signal in the brand; body copy is never tracked and never uppercase.

Minimums: 24px type in a 1920×1080 slide, 12pt in print, 44px hit targets on touch surfaces. All three faces are Google-hosted substitutions until licensed files arrive — the swap happens in tokens/fonts.css alone.

05 · Illustration

The painted hand

Saturated fields held inside a drawn contour, the paper reading through the lighter passages, nothing opaque white, nothing greyed to recede. The full plate — 23 places, figures and still lifes — mounts on toned stock with a 5px unpainted margin, so it never floats.

A painting opens the site; a photograph never does. Collection tiles are paintings (a tile is a place); product tiles and galleries are photographs. The full image-jobs table is at guidelines/image-jobs.html; the photography route itself is still an open decision, documented with four costed options in the specification.

05 · Illustration

Motifs are circles, patterns are squares

The six lifted details — decoration with provenance

Each is cut out of a finished painting and belongs to a place — using one is a claim about which place a page is about. Minimum 40px, one per page, never redrawn, never a control.

Three patterns, three strengths

Full strength for packaging and one hero band; 30% for section grounds; 8% behind long prose — applied as a ::before layer so the opacity never touches the text. Never two patterns on one screen.

05c · Illustration

The drawn register

  • The third job of the one handEight bench drawings on a 96px grid at the icons' 2px carbon rule, one pigment accent each. Not paintings — they claim no provenance from the plates.
  • Service, not romanceEmpty states, care and sizing instructions, transactional email. A drawing never opens a page and never sits beside a painted plate — the painting wins.
  • Words beside itLike an icon, a drawing never carries meaning alone: the annotation line under it says the thing plainly. "All twelve gone."
  • The motif law holdsNo drawn octopus, no drawn sun, no drawn spiral. Motifs stay paintings; drawings depict objects from the bench and the shop.

06 · Iconography

Sixteen marks, one construction

  • Construction24px grid, 16px keystone, the system's 2px carbon rule: butt caps, mitre joins, square corners, currentColor. One deliberate solid — the seal's centre dot.
  • SizesRendered at 24px, or 16px in the clerical register; never above 40px.
  • Never alone, never decoratingAn icon never carries meaning alone — the word sits beside it — and an icon never decorates. Motifs stay paintings; icons do the interface jobs; the two never trade.

07 · Shape, space, motion

Square, spacious, nearly still

Edge & depth
2px carbon — section break
1px hairline — row division
Flat pigment field

Corner radius is zero, everywhere. No shadows, no blur — the one exception is a gradient scrim over a hero painting so white type stays legible.

Space — 4px base

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128 · 176. Page margins are deliberately large — the paintings are surrounded by unpainted paper, and the layout should be too. Prose measures 62ch.

Motion — from print
instant80ms
fast140ms
base220ms
slow420ms
page700ms

Opacity and position only. No bounce, no spring, no scale-on-hover, no parallax. Reduced motion collapses every duration to zero. Focus is a 2px deep-water outline, offset 3px, never removed.

Twenty React components in components/ — core, content, forms, feedback, navigation — each with a typed interface and a .prompt.md contract for agent use, so generated pages inherit the rules.

  • Behaviour is part of the specDialogs trap focus, close on Escape and return focus to the opener. Tabs run the automatic-activation keyboard pattern. Errors are spoken — aria-invalid, described-by wiring, rust ink at 7.25:1 — never just painted.
  • Hover darkensOne step, the same direction as press. Nothing scales, nothing lifts, nothing casts.
  • The gate holds the lineEvery colour role a component uses is a measured pair in tools/check_contrast.py; a token edit that breaks one fails the build.

09 · In use

Six channels, two products, one system

The flagship case across six channels — post, story, email, run card, care card and line sheet — every one composed from the same tokens, registers and rules.

And a second product to prove the point: a harbourside taverna website built with nothing reskinned (cases/taverna-home.html). The palette came from the paintings; everything else carried unchanged.
