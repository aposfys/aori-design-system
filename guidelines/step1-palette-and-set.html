03

Illustrations — the pastel hand

Eighteen plates at assets/illustrations/, each one the toned-paper variant. The three I recommended cutting — beach, generations, bench — are not here; say the word and I will bring any of them back. Every plate is mounted on --stock-100 with the 5px unpainted margin the Plate component draws.

04

Motifs — the ink hand

Seven painted marks at assets/motifs/. The harder contour survives being shrunk, which is the whole reason this set is track B. These do the job an icon set would do elsewhere; the eight code-drawn SVG motifs are still in place underneath them until the templates are rebuilt.

06

The ground — receipt stock

Three printed grounds at assets/textures/, drawn to the sampled palette. Ledger sheet is the one I would make the default for anything administrative; grid is the quiet one for forms and long prose; carbon copy is loud and belongs on one page of a document, not on a website. These are procedural drafts — a real scan drops straight in over them.
