The real artwork changes
the whole system

The 81 files you just sent are painted in teal, terracotta and saffron on toned paper. The system in this project is painted in lime, wine and ochre. One of them has to move, and it should be the tokens — the art is the brand. Everything below is a proposal. Pick per section and I will build the final set.

01

Two style tracks — pick one

Every subject arrived in two hands. Track A is oil pastel on toned paper: soft edge, visible tooth, colour sitting in the paper. Track B is ink line with watercolour wash: hard contour, white ground, more graphic. Both are good. Mixing them in one storefront will read as two brands, so the system should commit — or commit A for landscape and B for motifs, which is my recommendation.

Patterns

Three repeats. They are strong but loud — a pattern at full strength will eat a product photograph. Proposal: keep all three, and define three strengths in the tokens — full (packaging, a single hero band), 30% over paper (section grounds), and 8% (behind long prose). 07

The name

AORI is fine but thin: it does not mean anything in Greek, it has no place attached to it, and the one drawn wordmark reverses the R, which reads as a mistake rather than a decision. Five directions, all sayable in Greek and English, all attached to something real on the island.

ÍDI
Ίδη
The mountain's older name — Ida, the peak the whole system is already built around. Two syllables, sets beautifully in caps, and the accent gives the wordmark its one deliberate mark. My recommendation.
LIVYKÓ
Λιβυκό
The Libyan Sea, off the south coast. Already a collection name; promoting it makes the brand about the water rather than the rock. Long in Latin.
AORI
—
Keep it. Costs nothing, but stays meaningless — and the reversed R has to go either way.
KRÓKOS
Κρόκος
Saffron. Warm, agricultural, ties to the saffron that is already the highlight colour. Risks reading as a food brand.
ORÍ
Ορί
From όρος, mountain — closest to the current sound, so the least disruptive change. Slightly invented.

09

What I do once you answer

In order, and nothing before you pick.

  1. Rewrite tokens/colors.css to the sampled palette, and every specimen card with it.
  2. Move the chosen variants into assets/illustrations/, assets/motifs/, assets/patterns/ under clean names, and delete the code-drawn SVGs they replace.
  3. Ship the chosen ground as assets/textures/ plus --surface-ledger / --pattern-* tokens at the three strengths.
  4. Redraw the chosen mark as SVG; wire it into both templates and the thumbnail.
  5. Rename throughout if the name changes — readme, SKILL.md, cards, templates, thumbnail.
  6. Then the storefront (home + product) and the two templates, on the new palette.
