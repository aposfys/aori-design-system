AORI · Logotype · Usage

One mark,
painted once

The wordmark is a vector trace of painted lettering and is the only mark. It is never re-set in type, never redrawn, and no second mark may be invented. The five painted emblems sit subordinate to it — an emblem may seal a box or close a page, but it never replaces the name.

01

Clear space

The unit is X — the cap height of the A. Nothing enters a margin of one X on all four sides: no type, no rule, no motif, no edge of page. On packaging and social crops, where space is tight, the margin may fall to ½X but never less.

02

Minimum sizes

120px — digital minimum
220px — hero and masthead

In print the floor is 30 mm wide. Below 120px the painted edge breaks up; there, and only there — favicons, avatars — the reversed-R monogram emblem stands in. Type-set letters never substitute for the mark at any size above 28px.

03

Placement & colourways

Four colourways, each also cut with the macron for the AŌRI styling. The choice is decided by the ground, never by taste:

Ink on paper — the default, everywhere
Terracotta on stock — packaging, cards
Paper on pigment — hero bands only
Paper on carbon — the media kit
  • On a paintingNever directly. The mark sits on a plate — a paper or stock field with its own clear space — laid over or beside the painting.
  • On a photographNever. A photograph shows a piece; the mark stays on the layout, not on the image.
  • One per surfaceA page, a post, a box face carries the mark once. The macron cut and the plain cut never appear together.
